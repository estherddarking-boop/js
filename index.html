Greenfield Local Hub

Greenfield Local Hub (GLH) is a cooperative made up of local farmers and independent food producers who work together to bring high‑quality, locally sourced products to their community. GLH operates as both a retail outlet and a support network, giving producers a shared platform to sell their goods, promote sustainable practices, and manage their operations more efficiently.
